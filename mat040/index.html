MAT040 Beginning Algebra

Course notes by Leonardo Pierre, with interactive examples. Every numbered example gives you new numbers each time you open the page, and checks your answer as soon as you submit it.

How the examples work

  • Type your answer in the box and press Enter or the Check button.
  • Fractions can be typed like 3/4, mixed numbers like 1 3/4, and decimals like 0.75.
  • Use ^ for exponents (x^2), and <=, >=, inf for ≤, ≥, ∞.
  • Stuck? Show worked solution explains the steps, and New numbers gives you a fresh version of the same problem.
  • Each chapter ends with a section for worked-example videos.
