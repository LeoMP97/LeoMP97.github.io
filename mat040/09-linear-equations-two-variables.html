Ordered Pairs and Quadrants

An ordered pair (x,y)(x,y)(x,y) names a point. The first number is the xxx-coordinate and the second is the yyy-coordinate.

To plot (2,3)(2,3)(2,3):  1. Start at the origin.  2. Move across the xxx-axis.  3. Move up or down the yyy-axis.

The axes divide the plane into four quadrants, labeled counterclockwise starting at the upper right.

Solutions to Linear Equations in Two Variables

In y=−2x+3y=-2x+3y=−2x+3, xxx is the independent variable and yyy is the dependent variable: the value of yyy depends on the value of xxx.

To find possible solutions to an equation with two variables, get the variables on opposite sides of the equal sign, get the dependent variable by itself, then plug in a number for xxx and find the corresponding yyy value.

Intercepts

An intercept is where the graphed line crosses an axis.
The xxx-intercept is where the line crosses the xxx-axis, and where y=0y=0y=0.
The yyy-intercept is where the line crosses the yyy-axis, and where x=0x=0x=0.

Here the yyy-intercept is (0,1)(0,1)(0,1) and the xxx-intercept is (−1,0)(-1,0)(−1,0). So y=1y=1y=1 when x=0x=0x=0 and x=−1x=-1x=−1 when y=0y=0y=0.

Example. Find the intercepts of 3x−2y=63x-2y=63x−2y=6. First solve for 2y2y2y: 3x−6=2y3x-6=2y3x−6=2y.

xxx-intercept (set y=0y=0y=0): 3x−6=2(0)=0 → 3x=6 → x=63=23x-6=2(0)=0\ \to\ 3x=6\ \to\ x=\dfrac63=23x−6=2(0)=0 → 3x=6 → x=36​=2. The xxx-intercept is (2,0)(2,0)(2,0).

yyy-intercept (set x=0x=0x=0): 3(0)−6=2y → −6=2y → y=−33(0)-6=2y\ \to\ -6=2y\ \to\ y=-33(0)−6=2y → −6=2y → y=−3. The yyy-intercept is (0,−3)(0,-3)(0,−3).

Example. Find the intercepts of −x+2y=4-x+2y=4−x+2y=4. Then 2y=4+x2y=4+x2y=4+x.

xxx-intercept: 0=4+x → x=−40=4+x\ \to\ x=-40=4+x → x=−4, so (−4,0)(-4,0)(−4,0).
yyy-intercept: 2y=4+0 → y=22y=4+0\ \to\ y=22y=4+0 → y=2, so (0,2)(0,2)(0,2).
