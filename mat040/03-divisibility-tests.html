Divisibility Tests

The numbered examples below are interactive: the numbers change every time you load the page. Type your answer, press Enter or Check, and use New numbers to practice again.

Another way to say that 375375375 is a multiple of 555 is to say that 375375375 is divisible by 555. In fact 375÷5=75375\div5=75375÷5=75, so 375=5⋅75375=5\cdot75375=5⋅75. On the other hand 10,51910{,}51910,519 is not a multiple of 333. When we divide 10,51910{,}51910,519 by 333 we do not get a counting number, so 10,51910{,}51910,519 is not divisible by 333.

If a number mmm is a multiple of nnn, then we say that mmm is divisible by nnn.

Instead of dividing, we can use the following tests for some of the counting numbers between one and ten.

A number is divisible by
222if the last digit is 000, 222, 444, 666 or 888
333if the sum of the digits is divisible by 333
444if the last two digits are divisible by 444
555if the last digit is 000 or 555
666if the number is divisible by both 222 and 333
888if the last three digits are divisible by 888
999if the sum of the digits is divisible by 999
101010if the last digit is 000
