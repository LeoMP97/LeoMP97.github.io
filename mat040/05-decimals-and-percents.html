Mixed Notation

Back when we began fractions, we used shaded boxes to visualize them. Consider the improper fraction 74\frac7447​. Each box is broken into four pieces, so we need two boxes to shade seven pieces:

  

We get one full box and one box that is 34\frac3443​ shaded. That is

74=44+34=1+34\frac74=\frac44+\frac34=1+\frac3447​=44​+43​=1+43​

and we write 74=134\dfrac74=1\dfrac3447​=143​.

We can turn improper fractions into a combination of a whole number and a proper fraction. This is called mixed notation (a mixed number).

Let’s say we have the improper fraction 136\frac{13}6613​. We break the numerator into as many copies of the denominator as we can:

136→66+76→66+66+16→1+1+16=216\frac{13}6\to\frac66+\frac76\to\frac66+\frac66+\frac16\to1+1+\frac16=2\frac16613​→66​+67​→66​+66​+61​→1+1+61​=261​

So 136=216\dfrac{13}6=2\dfrac16613​=261​.

and the three digits after the decimal point come from 1+2=31+2=31+2=3 digits.
